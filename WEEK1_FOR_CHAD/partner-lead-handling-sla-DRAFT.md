Fourteen leads where ten were never called and half never got an email doesn't tell me anything at all. That's what I want to stop — spending months building traffic and then not knowing whether the thing on the other end works.
>

## What I'd put in writing between you two

Short, so it can't be argued about later. Adjust the hour if you changed it above.

| | Term |
|---|---|
| **First contact** | Within one business hour of the lead arriving, during business hours. Leads arriving outside business hours get contacted the next business morning. |
| **Attempts before "lost"** | At least three attempts across at least three different days, at least two different times of day, before a lead is marked lost. |
| **Outcome logged** | Within seven days of the lead arriving. One of: qualified, not qualified, appointment set, sold, lost. |
| **Reason required** | On "not qualified" and "lost" only. One line. |
| **Where** | The referrals page on the dashboard. |
| **What Chad owes** | Lead delivered with utility, monthly bill bracket, credit self-report, city and the page they came from. Owner alert fires the moment it lands. |
| **Review** | Both of you look at the log monthly. If close rate is zero after twenty worked leads, the problem is the leads and that's Chad's to fix. |

That last row matters and it cuts both ways. It's the line that stops this becoming an argument about whose fault it is — twenty worked leads with a real log settles it either way.
